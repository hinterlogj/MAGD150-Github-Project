# MAGD150-Github-Project

#### Assignment 2
This assignment explored making shapes and using colors to make a cohesive composition. I went with a space theme.

#### Assignment 4
This sketch was to practice creating movement when the mouse and a key is pressed. Multiple circles are drawn using a for loop. When you press or hold the "r" key, the middle circle fills in red. When the mouse is pressed or held down all the circles fill in pink. 

#### Assignment 7
This assignment was to work on making a shape out of other shapes, and then having it move in different ways such as scale, rotate, angle, and translate. Push and pop are used to organize it and make certain shapes do certain moves. I made my weather themed with moving clouds and a sun.
